Kućni red / House Rules

Dobro došli! Želimo vam ugodan i prijatan boravak.
Welcome! We wish you a pleasant and enjoyable stay.

🇷🇸 Kućni red

  1. PRIJAVA / ODJAVA
    Prijava (Check-in) je od 15:00, a odjava (Check-out) do 11:00, osim ako nije drugačije dogovoreno.
  2. PRIJAVLJENI GOSTI
    U apartmanu mogu boraviti isključivo prijavljeni gosti.
  3. ZABRANE
    Zabave, proslave i okupljanja nisu dozvoljeni.
  4. KUĆNI MIR
    Molimo vas da poštujete kućni mir od 22:00 do 07:00 i izbjegavate buku koja bi mogla ometati ostale stanare.
  5. PUŠENJE
    Pušenje nije dozvoljeno u apartmanu. Pušenje je dozvoljeno isključivo na mjestima predviđenim za tu namjenu.
  6. OBUĆA
    Molimo vas da u apartmanu ne nosite obuću koju koristite izvan apartmana. Pri ulasku izujte obuću i odložite je u cipelarnik. Za korištenje su obezbijeđene čiste papuče namijenjene isključivo za korištenje u apartmanu.
  7. INVENTAR I OPREMA
    Molimo vas da pažljivo koristite sav inventar i opremu. Ukoliko primijetite kvar ili dođe do oštećenja, obavijestite domaćina što je prije moguće.
  8. PRILIKOM IZLASKA
    Prilikom izlaska iz apartmana ugasite rasvjetu, klima uređaj i ostale električne uređaje, zaključajte vrata i zatvorite prozore.
  9. PARKING
    Molimo vas da vozilo parkirate na našem parkingu na način da parking ostane raspoloživ za ostale goste i korisnike naše zgrade.
  10. ČISTOĆA
    Molimo vas da apartman ostavite urednim i da smeće odložite u predviđene kontejnere prije odlaska.
  11. KLJUČEVI
    Ključeve čuvajte pažljivo. Gubitak ključeva može prouzrokovati dodatne troškove.
  12. KONTAKT
    U slučaju hitne potrebe ili bilo kakvog pitanja, slobodno kontaktirajte domaćina – rado ćemo vam pomoći.

🇬🇧 House Rules

  1. CHECK-IN / CHECK-OUT
    Check-in is from 15:00, and check-out is until 11:00, unless otherwise agreed.
  2. REGISTERED GUESTS ONLY
    Only guests who are registered are allowed to stay in the apartment.
  3. NO PARTIES OR EVENTS
    Parties, celebrations and gatherings are not allowed.
  4. QUIET HOURS
    Please respect quiet hours from 22:00 to 07:00 and avoid noise that may disturb other residents.
  5. NO SMOKING
    Smoking is not allowed inside the apartment. Smoking is allowed only in designated areas.
  6. SHOES
    Please do not wear outdoor shoes inside the apartment. At the entrance, please remove your shoes and place them in the shoe cabinet. Clean slippers are provided for your comfort and are intended for indoor use only.
  7. INVENTORY & EQUIPMENT
    Please treat all inventory and equipment with care. If you notice any damage or malfunction, please inform the host as soon as possible.
  8. WHEN LEAVING
    When leaving the apartment, please turn off the lights, air conditioning and other electrical appliances, lock the door and close the windows.
  9. PARKING
    Please park your vehicle on our parking area in a way that keeps the parking available for other guests and building users.
  10. CLEANLINESS
    Please leave the apartment tidy and dispose of the trash in the provided containers before check-out.
  11. KEYS
    Please keep the keys carefully. Loss of keys may result in additional charges.
  12. CONTACT
    In case of an emergency or if you have any questions, feel free to contact the host – we will be happy to assist you.

⚠️ VAŽNA NAPOMENA / IMPORTANT NOTICE

SR: Domaćin živi u prizemlju ovog objekta. Ukoliko gosti svojim ponašanjem prouzrokuju buku, lupanje ili galamu u bilo koje doba dana, domaćin ima pravo da uđe u apartman radi pregleda stanja stvari i utvrđivanja eventualne učinjene štete.
Svaka šteta na stvarima i opremi u apartmanu biće odmah naplaćena od gosta koji ju je učinio.

EN: The host lives on the ground floor of this building. If guests cause noise, loud talking, banging or any disturbance at any time of the day, the host has the right to enter the apartment to inspect the condition of the property and assess any potential damage.
Any damage to items and equipment in the apartment will be charged to the guest responsible.
